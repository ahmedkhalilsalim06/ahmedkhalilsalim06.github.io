What was searched, and what isn't here

The sweep covered the departmental faculty list, all twenty personal ~user folders it links to, each instructor's course subfolders, and a Wayback CDX sweep of the whole fen.bilkent.edu.tr domain filtered on the course numbers.

Both courses stopped being taught publicly

The newest MATH 110 archive is Fall 2014 and the newest MATH 210 archive is Spring 2016. Neither code appears in recent Bilkent offerings, so nothing more is coming.

Nothing was worked around

Everything here was posted publicly by the instructor. No access control was bypassed; the archived copies are snapshots of pages that were openly reachable when the crawler visited. Material behind Moodle or STARS stays there — reach it with your own student login on the campus network or VPN.

Use these the way they were meant

These are study materials. Past papers are for practising against; check your course's own academic-honesty policy before using anything in graded work, and note that syllabi and grading schemes shown here belong to old semesters and no longer apply.
