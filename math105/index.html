MATH 105

Introduction to Calculus I

The calculus course for economics and business students — limits, differentiation, optimisation, curve sketching, applied to economic problems. Everything below was posted by Salih Karadağ and has since vanished from the university server; every link is an Internet Archive recovery.

What was searched, and what isn't here

The sweep covered the mathematics faculty list and all twenty personal ~user folders it links to, each instructor’s course subfolders, and a Wayback CDX sweep of the whole fen.bilkent.edu.tr domain filtered on the course number.

One instructor ever posted MATH 105 publicly

No other Bilkent mathematics folder contains a single MATH 105 file. This is not a gap in the search — it is the whole public record for the course.

No midterms or finals for MATH 105

Only the two quizzes were posted. The solved midterm in the MATH 106 group is the nearest thing to a full past paper from this instructor, which is why it is included.

Nothing after 2014

The newest file of any kind is a 2014 MATH 106 quiz. Recent sections run entirely on Moodle and STARS — reach those with your own student login on the campus network or VPN.

Use these the way they were meant

These are study materials. Check your section’s academic-honesty policy before using anything in graded work, and note that the syllabi and grading schemes here belong to old semesters.
