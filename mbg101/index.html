What is not here, and why

  • No MBG 101 exam has ever been posted publicly. Not a midterm, not a final, not a quiz — in any year, by any instructor, under either course code. I went back a second time for this one: probed 174 exam-shaped filenames against Konu’s live folder, ran a Wayback sweep for exam-, midterm-, final- and quiz-named files across every MBG-linked folder on fen.bilkent.edu.tr, and walked the old department’s per-faculty ~bilmbg/people/facmem/ tree. The only exam paper any MBG instructor left in public is Konu’s MBG 210 take-home final, listed below under Genetics.
  • The 1997 course outline tells you the shape of the exams — two midterms at 25% each, final at 40%, homework and attendance 10% — but links no papers.
  • One file was found and deliberately not linked: a makeup-exam results spreadsheet from Akçalı’s 2005 section. It is a list of named students’ grades, not course material.
  • The lecture index is filed as mbg105lectures.htm even though the page itself is headed MBG-101. That mismatch is why no search engine surfaces it, and it is the single reason this section has any content at all.
  • Seven files are gone entirely — Konu’s Lecture 5a, Lecture 5b, Stereoisomers and Development, and Akçalı’s MBG 110 syllabus and three lecture decks. Deleted from the server and never crawled, so no copy exists anywhere. They still appear as links on their course pages; do not chase them.
  • Checked and empty: the current MBG department site, all eleven current faculty profiles, the old departmental ~bilmbg folder including its per-course pages, and Wayback sweeps of both mbg.bilkent.edu.tr and fen.bilkent.edu.tr. The department’s public files are internship forms and alumni lists.
  • Current sections run on Moodle — enrolled-student access through your Bilkent account, which is where anything recent will be.
