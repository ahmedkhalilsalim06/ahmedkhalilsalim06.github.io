Molecules of life, the central dogma from DNA to protein, handling nucleic acids and proteins, and an introduction to genetics, immunology, development and cancer. The course ran as MBG 110 before it became MBG 101, so older Biology I material appears under that code. Only lecture material survives in public. Catalog entry.

Everything below was posted publicly by the course’s instructors. LIVE links go to the file on the Bilkent server. ARCHIVE links open the copy the Internet Archive took while the page was public. Grade lists, attendance sheets, rosters and exam-room lists were left out because they name students.

Use these for practice, not submission. Old solutions show how a problem was graded in that year. Handing them in breaks Bilkent’s academic-integrity rules.

Current material lives on Moodle. For this semester’s slides and exams, use Moodle (from campus or the university VPN). STARS holds the official syllabus for every section since 2010.
