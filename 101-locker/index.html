Where the public record stops

  • No solutions for Tekman’s questions. He posts the question and the figure, never the answer — that is a deliberate teaching choice, not a gap in this archive. For worked solutions, use Gürses (Spring 2011) and Degtyarev.
  • Fall 2007 is the odd one out — posted as four whole-paper PDFs instead of per-question pages, and it does include solutions.
  • A few Özkan files are gone for good. Three .doc homework files and several of her quiz pages were never crawled before the folder was deleted.
  • Recent quizzes rotate. The quizzes page carries the current term only — Fall 2025 as of this build. Earlier terms’ quiz PDFs still sit in the same folder but are no longer linked from anywhere.
  • Checked and empty: the twenty personal folders of every current Mathematics faculty member, the department course index, and a Wayback sweep of the whole fen.bilkent.edu.tr domain. Nothing else public exists for MATH 101.

What is not here, and why

  • Seven papers is the whole public record. Salzner’s own “old exams” index lists exactly these seven files — nothing was quietly dropped from this page.
  • No solutions, ever. The exams page linked papers only. No key was posted for any of them, and none was archived.
  • No current material. Every present-day CHEM 101 section runs on Moodle. That is enrolled-student access through your Bilkent account — it is not something this page can or should route around.
  • Checked and empty: the personal folders of all twelve current Chemistry faculty, the department site, the departmental curricula pages, and a Wayback sweep of both chem.bilkent.edu.tr and fen.bilkent.edu.tr for anything matching CHEM 101. The only other chemistry teaching material in public is Erman Bengü’s CHEM 201 folder, a different course.
  • The newest CHEM 101 file in public dates from 2005. That is where the trail ends.

What is not here, and why

  • No MBG 101 exam has ever been posted publicly. Not a midterm, not a final, not a quiz — in any year, by any instructor, under either course code. I went back a second time for this one: probed 174 exam-shaped filenames against Konu’s live folder, ran a Wayback sweep for exam-, midterm-, final- and quiz-named files across every MBG-linked folder on fen.bilkent.edu.tr, and walked the old department’s per-faculty ~bilmbg/people/facmem/ tree. The only exam paper any MBG instructor left in public is Konu’s MBG 210 take-home final, listed below under Genetics.
  • The 1997 course outline tells you the shape of the exams — two midterms at 25% each, final at 40%, homework and attendance 10% — but links no papers.
  • One file was found and deliberately not linked: a makeup-exam results spreadsheet from Akçalı’s 2005 section. It is a list of named students’ grades, not course material.
  • The lecture index is filed as mbg105lectures.htm even though the page itself is headed MBG-101. That mismatch is why no search engine surfaces it, and it is the single reason this section has any content at all.
  • Seven files are gone entirely — Konu’s Lecture 5a, Lecture 5b, Stereoisomers and Development, and Akçalı’s MBG 110 syllabus and three lecture decks. Deleted from the server and never crawled, so no copy exists anywhere. They still appear as links on their course pages; do not chase them.
  • Checked and empty: the current MBG department site, all eleven current faculty profiles, the old departmental ~bilmbg folder including its per-course pages, and Wayback sweeps of both mbg.bilkent.edu.tr and fen.bilkent.edu.tr. The department’s public files are internship forms and alumni lists.
  • Current sections run on Moodle — enrolled-student access through your Bilkent account, which is where anything recent will be.

Course description. MBG 101 Biology I, 4 credits / 6.5 ECTS: molecules of life, the central dogma from DNA to protein, manipulation and analysis of nucleic acids and proteins, and an introduction to genetics, immunology, development and cancer. Official catalog entry · Current MBG offerings
