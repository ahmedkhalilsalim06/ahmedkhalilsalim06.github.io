What was searched, and what isn't here

The sweep covered the economics faculty list and all twenty-two personal sites it links to, the department domain, a Wayback CDX sweep of econ.bilkent.edu.tr and of each Bilkent-hosted faculty subdomain, and filename probing on the folders that turned up.

No solved exam papers exist

Only two real ECON 101 papers were ever posted — a midterm 1 and a midterm 2 with answers. There is no public final. The two practice sets above are what the course gave out instead, and they are the closest thing to a past-paper bank you will find.

The other instructors publish research, not teaching

Berument’s site is entirely econometrics (ECON 301/302/317). Taşkın’s holds ECON 302 readings. Kızılkaya’s covers ECON 222. Hasker publishes ECON 107, 203, 204 and 439 — no 101. The rest host on Google Sites with no course files at all.

Nothing was worked around

Every file here was posted publicly by the instructor. No access control was bypassed. Current-semester material lives on Moodle and STARS — reach it with your own student login.

Use these the way they were meant

These are study materials. Check your section’s academic-honesty policy before using anything in graded work, and note that the syllabi and grading schemes here belong to old semesters.
