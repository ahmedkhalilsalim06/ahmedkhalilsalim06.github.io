Called “Introduction to Economics I” in older catalogs. Microeconomic principles: supply and demand, elasticity, consumer and producer theory, market structures and welfare. Five instructors have posted ECON 101 material publicly: Taner Yiğit’s site is still live, and the folders of Çağla Ökten, Osman Zaim, Selin Sayek Böke and Tarık Kara survive only in the Internet Archive. Catalog entry.

Everything below was posted publicly by the course’s instructors. LIVE links go to the file on the Bilkent server. ARCHIVE links open the copy the Internet Archive took while the page was public. Grade lists, attendance sheets, rosters and exam-room lists were left out because they name students.

Use these for practice, not submission. Old solutions show how a problem was graded in that year. Handing them in breaks Bilkent’s academic-integrity rules.

Current material lives on Moodle. For this semester’s slides and exams, use Moodle (from campus or the university VPN). STARS holds the official syllabus for every section since 2010.

Linked but lost

  • Taner Yiğit never posted a final exam; his site’s /intro/ folder was probed with about 70 filename variants and nothing beyond the linked files exists.

What was searched

  • Instructor sites: tyigit.bilkent.edu.tr (live), econ.bilkent.edu.tr (research papers only), and the old www.bilkent.edu.tr/~user folders
  • Wayback CDX: a host query on www.bilkent.edu.tr filtered for econ101, which named all five instructors’ folders, then a full listing of each folder
  • Filename probing: about 70 variants against tyigit.bilkent.edu.tr/intro/
  • Left out: Yiğit’s macroeconomics files (a different course)
  • Student repos (25 Sep 2026): about 1,430 public GitHub repositories whose name, description or topics mention Bilkent were collected, and the full file lists of about 580 of them (every coursework repo and monorepo among them) were searched, along with code-name searches for this course. Nothing for ECON 101 (one repo holds ECON 108 homework, a different course).
