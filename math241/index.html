Taught by the EEE department, not Mathematics: differential equations and linear algebra for engineers, with weekly quizzes and MATLAB. Textbook for years: Greenberg, Advanced Engineering Mathematics. Catalog entry.

Everything below was posted publicly by the course’s instructors and opens directly, with no account or payment. LIVE links go to the file on the Bilkent server. ARCHIVE links open the copy the Internet Archive took while the page was public. Grade lists, attendance sheets, rosters and seating plans were left out because they name students.

Use these for practice, not submission. Old solutions show how a problem was graded in that year. Handing them in, or reusing lab reports, breaks Bilkent’s academic-integrity rules.

Current material lives on Moodle. For this semester’s slides and exams, use Moodle (from campus or the university VPN). STARS holds the official syllabus for every section since 2010.
