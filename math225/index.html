What was searched

  • Bilkent catalog entry (c03225) and the STARS term list — the course has run every term since 2010-11, with summer sections.
  • Every Mathematics-department personal folder that mentions 225: ~yosum (Kurtulmaz), ~mugan, ~gurses, ~gozkan, ~aydan, ~kocatepe, ~aurelian, ~yurekli.
  • The EEE department's own MATH 225 sections: ~math225 and ~eeweb/math225 on both www.ee and kilyos.ee, plus ~ider's Fall 2005 course folder.
  • Live re-checks of all 56 candidate files: 52 still answer 200 on the instructors' own servers, so they are linked live rather than through the archive.
  • Wayback CDX sweeps of fen.bilkent.edu.tr, ee.bilkent.edu.tr and kilyos.ee.bilkent.edu.tr for every URL containing "225".
  • GitHub, GitLab and Codeberg student-repo sweep (2026-09-19): roughly 3,500 Bilkent-linked repositories plus 1,000 repos named math225 — no MATH 225 exam or quiz paper on any open code host. The only near misses are two practice sheets in MAKman1/Bilkent-CS-Curriculum (MATH225/Practice1 and Practice2), which are not real papers.

Linked but lost

  • Kurtulmaz's Spring 2008-09 midterm-2 solution sheet (20090502111432523.pdf): still linked from his exam index page, but the file is gone from the server and was never archived.
  • Kurtulmaz's 2009, 2010 and 2011 Fall course pages (2009Fall.Math225.htm and siblings) — linked, never crawled.
  • Özkan's homework weeks 2–8, 10, 11 and 13 — only weeks 1, 9, 12 and 14 were ever archived.
  • ~yurekli/math/225 (Doğan Yürekli, 2005): the folder structure was archived but every file inside it redirects and no content survives.
  • Muğan's 'ugurcan.htm' problem page and the LR/discoII and LR/seriesIII note sets — 404 at crawl time.

Deliberately left out

  • Left out on purpose: every grade and exam-room page that names students — the ~math225/grades/ spreadsheets on the EEE server, Muğan's Grades-posted PDFs, Gürses's ORTALAMA2.xls, Özkan's quiz-section and name-range lists, and Pamir's exam-place pages.
