Python for first-year students outside Computer Engineering: expressions and branching, loops, functions, files and modules, lists and dictionaries, classes and inheritance, searching and sorting, then NumPy and matplotlib. Four credits with a weekly four-hour lab. Taught since Fall 2018 by Aynur Dayanık, İpek Sözen and Lori Russell Dağ, with Can Kültür (Fall 2022) and Mustafa Değerli (since 2025). Catalog entry.

HOSTED files are copies from the site owner’s own Fall 2021–22 course folder: the ten lab sheets, the data files they read, and one student’s solutions with the name removed from files and paths. LIVE links go to Bilkent servers.

Use these for practice, not submission. Lab questions are reused across terms. Handing in someone else’s solution breaks Bilkent’s academic-integrity rules.

Current material lives on Moodle. For this semester’s labs, slides and exams, use Moodle (from campus or the university VPN).
