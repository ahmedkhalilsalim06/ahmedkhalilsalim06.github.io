CS 223

Digital Design

Everything public for CS 223, pulled from five instructors' folders — Will Sawyer, Özcan Öztürk, Mehmet Baray and two teaching assistants — spanning 1997 to 2015. The exams, slides and labs are archive copies: both surviving course pages are live, but their files were deleted from the server.

How to read the badges: live still sits on the instructor's own server; archive is a Wayback Machine copy of a page that was publicly reachable when it was crawled. Everything here opens for anyone, with no login and no payment. Use it for your own study and follow the course's academic-honesty policy.

What was searched

  • Bilkent catalog entry (c11223) and the STARS section list for every term from 2014-15 to 2026-27.
  • Every CS-department personal folder that mentions 223: ~will (Will Sawyer), ~ozturk, ~baray, ~oonder, ~sermetcan, ~ubora, ~ayavuz, ~busra, ~bastan, ~guvenir, ~s.rahimzadeh.
  • Live checks of every file linked from the surviving course pages — on ~will and ~ozturk only the index pages still answer; the files themselves are gone, so the archived copies are linked instead.
  • Wayback CDX sweeps of cs.bilkent.edu.tr and www.cs.bilkent.edu.tr for every URL containing "223" (1,229 captures), plus a full prefix enumeration of ~will/courses/CS223/ (349 captures).
  • GitHub, GitLab and Codeberg student-repo sweep (2026-09-19): file trees of roughly 3,500 Bilkent-linked repositories and 1,000 repos named cs223 — no CS 223 exam or quiz paper exists on any open code host.

Linked but lost

  • FinalExam_2006.doc and FinalExam_2009.doc — linked from the past-exam pages, never crawled.
  • Öztürk's ManoKimeSlides.html (extra Mano/Kime slide set) and PetervrlK.pdf — linked, never archived.
  • Sawyer's homework 9 and 10 sheets, Homeworks/hw10_sol.htm, Labs lab6_sec1–4 and PEGASUS-rm.pdf.
  • The 2002–03 Baray notes sets 1 and 2 (cs223intro, cs223notes2) — the server answered 403 when the crawler called.

Deliberately left out

  • Left out on purpose: every grade, attendance and section list that names students — ~will/courses/CS223/Grades/*, examgrades*.htm, ~ozturk sec1–3.htm and sec1quiz.htm, ~busra and ~bastan section-grade pages, Baray's CS223Spring2012 MidtermGrades.pdf and the Quizsaps grade spreadsheet. Givone.pdf (the course textbook) is also left out — it is a commercial book, not course material the instructor wrote.
