The research-based alternative to HIST 200: each group picks a topic in Turkey’s history, works from primary sources, and writes up a paper in stages (proposal, preliminary reports, draft, presentation, final paper). Offered since Fall 2012. Catalog entry.

LIVE links go to Bilkent servers. REPO links are copies a student shared in a public GitHub repository, not checked against the official version. Group papers, proposals and anything else that names students were left out.

There are no past exams to find. No STARS syllabus since 2013–14 lists a midterm or final. The grade comes from the research proposal, one short quiz (since 2018), two preliminary reports, a presentation, peer review and the final term project.

Use these for orientation, not submission. Reusing another group’s paper breaks Bilkent’s academic-integrity rules. Current material lives on Moodle.
