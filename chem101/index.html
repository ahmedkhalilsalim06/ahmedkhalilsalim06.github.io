What is not here, and why

  • Seven papers is the whole public record. Salzner’s own “old exams” index lists exactly these seven files — nothing was quietly dropped from this page.
  • No solutions, ever. The exams page linked papers only. No key was posted for any of them, and none was archived.
  • No current material. Every present-day CHEM 101 section runs on Moodle. That is enrolled-student access through your Bilkent account — it is not something this page can or should route around.
  • Checked and empty: the personal folders of all twelve current Chemistry faculty, the department site, the departmental curricula pages, and a Wayback sweep of both chem.bilkent.edu.tr and fen.bilkent.edu.tr for anything matching CHEM 101. The only other chemistry teaching material in public is Erman Bengü’s CHEM 201 folder, a different course.
  • The newest CHEM 101 file in public dates from 2005. That is where the trail ends.
