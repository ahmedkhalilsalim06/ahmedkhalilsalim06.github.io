Everything here opens for anyone, with no login and no payment. Use it for your own study and follow the course's academic-honesty policy.
The lecture slides, code examples and handouts on the course page are not publicly downloadable: the page itself is public, but every file under ~saksoy/courses/cs211/ answers 403 Forbidden to anyone off campus. That is deliberate access control by the instructor, so this archive does not link copies of them. Open them from the campus network, over the university VPN, or from Moodle, where the course also posts its assignments.
