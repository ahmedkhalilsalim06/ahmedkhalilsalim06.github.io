HUM 111 Antiquity

Cultures, Civilizations and Ideas I

Every piece of HUM 111 material that has ever been posted in public, gathered in one place. That means the program's own site, the official STARS syllabi, instructors' old personal pages recovered from the Wayback Machine, and the few instructor handouts students kept in public GitHub repositories. Current sections run on Moodle, so nearly all real past papers date from 2001–2007.

Heads-up: most 2000–2004 material taught the Odyssey rather than the Iliad, and the Apology and Crito rather than the Republic. Freud and Gilgamesh have been on the list since the start.

What's missing, and why

Where the public record stops

The newest exam paper anywhere in public is Daren Hodson's take-home final from January 2007. After about 2013, instructors stopped posting on personal pages and moved to Moodle. Quizzes, recent finals and course readers stay there, behind a Bilkent login. The newest public course document is the 2025–26 general syllabus.

Linked by the instructor but lost (never archived, or already deleted when crawled)

  • Hodson, 2012–13: question sets for Freud, Gilgamesh, the Iliad, Antigone and the Republic, the Fall 2013 syllabus, the Göbekli Tepe homework and a sample quote-analysis paper
  • Bowe, Summer 2003: the final exam, the midterm assignment, the syllabus and the Republic readings. Spring 2004: the "tauroctony" handout
  • Gumpert, Fall 2001: study guides 2 and 8
  • Pelin Altan McLaren, Fall 2001 adjunct: summary and compare-contrast prompts, checklists, oral exam sheet
  • Old CCI text pages for Homer, Sophocles, Freud (glossary), Confucius, Locke and the 1001 Nights

Student repositories (added 20 September 2026)

File lists of about 23,000 public GitHub repositories (every repo whose name, description or README mentions Bilkent, plus all repos of 1,052 Bilkent students’ accounts), and GitLab and Codeberg. Only two students kept HUM 111 folders. One (ArdaTavusbay/BilkentCS, Fall 2021, Kimball’s section) holds the midterm-project instructions and exercise prompts now listed above, next to the student’s own essays. The other (MAKman1/Bilkent-CS-Curriculum) holds only the student’s own course project, which carries his name and ID, so it is not linked. No HUM 111 exam paper is in either.

Found, but deliberately not linked

These files hold named or ID-numbered student grades: grade sheets, class rosters, presentation grades and adjunct results. Also left out are full-text copies of copyrighted translations (a Turkish translation of Freud, and Plato's Apology).

Checked and empty

The CCI site's full sitemap (103 pages), every faculty profile on it, the old ~cci program site, 32 personal folders of past and present instructors, and a Wayback sweep of www.bilkent.edu.tr for HUM 111, Gilgamesh, Iliad, Antigone and Oedipus. Studocu, Course Hero and Scribd list HUM 111 uploads, but those are student uploads behind a login, so they aren't linked here.

Academic honesty. Everything here is old material that instructors posted in public, collected so you can see how the course is taught and practise with it. Midterm papers and take-home finals were graded individual work. Reusing a prompt's answer, or any part of another student's paper, falls under Bilkent's academic honesty rules.
