The first half of Bilkent’s core humanities sequence: Gilgamesh, Homer, Greek tragedy and lyric, Plato and Aristotle, read alongside Braudel and Freud. Every section follows a common general syllabus. Most instructor material is from 2000–2013 and survives only in the Internet Archive; the program’s current site holds the general syllabi. The adjunct language course that ran alongside HUM 111 in 2000–2002 is listed under Labs & tutorials. Official course description (CCI program).

Everything below was posted publicly by the course’s instructors, or shared by students on GitHub. LIVE links go to the file on the Bilkent server. ARCHIVE links open the copy the Internet Archive took while the page was public. REPO links are files a student shared in a public GitHub repository, not checked against the official version. Grade lists, attendance sheets, rosters and exam-room lists were left out because they name students.

Use these for practice, not submission. Old solutions show how a problem was graded in that year. Handing them in breaks Bilkent’s academic-integrity rules.

Current material lives on Moodle. Section syllabi for every term since 2010 are on STARS; find your own instructor and section there. Current readings and assignments are on Moodle.
