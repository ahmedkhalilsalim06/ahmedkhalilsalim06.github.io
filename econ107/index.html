Demand and supply, elasticity, consumer and firm behaviour, market structures and welfare. Taken by most non-economics majors. Grading since Spring 2025: two midterms (30% each), four quizzes or homeworks (20%), a final (20%) and a small participation bonus. Catalog entry.

Almost everything here comes from one instructor, Kevin Hasker, who posts every quiz and exam answer for his sections, and in 2025 also posted other instructors’ midterms for practice. LIVE links go to the file on the Bilkent server. Grade lists and exam-room assignments were left out because they name students.

Use these for practice, not submission. Solutions show how that instructor graded that year. Other sections use different quizzes and weights, so check your own section’s syllabus.

Your section’s material lives on Moodle. Instructors other than Hasker post only there (campus network or the university VPN).
