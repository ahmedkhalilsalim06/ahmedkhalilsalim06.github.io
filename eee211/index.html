Built around one project: designing and building the TRC-10, a 27 MHz HF radio transceiver. Passive parts, diodes, BJTs and op-amps, filters, audio and RF amplifiers, oscillators, mixers, antennas. Catalog entry.

Most of what is below was posted publicly by the course’s instructors or assistants, or shared by students on GitHub. LIVE links go to the file on the Bilkent server. ARCHIVE links open the copy the Internet Archive took while the page was public. HOSTED files are copies of past papers, handouts and Moodle files from the site owner’s own course folders, kept on this site. Completed lab reports had the student’s name and ID removed. REPO links are files a student shared in a public GitHub repository, not checked against the official version. Some are HTML pages whose scanned images were only partly captured, and those are marked. Grade lists, attendance sheets, rosters and seating plans were left out because they name students.

Use these for practice, not submission. Old solutions show how a problem was graded in that year. Handing them in, or reusing lab reports, breaks Bilkent’s academic-integrity rules.

Current material lives on Moodle. For this semester’s slides and exams, use Moodle (from campus or the university VPN). STARS holds the official syllabus for every section since 2010.
