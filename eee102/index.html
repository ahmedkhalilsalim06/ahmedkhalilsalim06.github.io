Number systems, Boolean algebra, combinational and sequential logic, HDL (ABEL, then VHDL) on FPGA boards. Prerequisite CS 101 or CS 115; 4 credits, 6.5 ECTS. Catalog entry.

Everything below was posted publicly by the course’s instructors, or shared by students on GitHub. LIVE links go to the file on the Bilkent server. ARCHIVE links open the copy the Internet Archive took while the page was public. REPO links are files a student shared in a public GitHub repository, not checked against the official version. HOSTED files are the site owner’s own copies of course handouts, kept on this site. Grade lists, attendance sheets, rosters and exam-room lists were left out because they name students.

Use these for practice, not submission. Old solutions show how a problem was graded in that year. Handing them in breaks Bilkent’s academic-integrity rules.

Current material lives on Moodle. For this semester’s slides and exams, use Moodle (from campus or the university VPN). STARS holds the official syllabus for every section since 2010.
