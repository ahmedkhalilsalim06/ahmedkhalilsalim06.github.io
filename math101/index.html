Where the public record stops

  • No solutions for Tekman’s questions. He posts the question and the figure, never the answer — that is a deliberate teaching choice, not a gap in this archive. For worked solutions, use Gürses (Spring 2011) and Degtyarev.
  • Fall 2007 is the odd one out — posted as four whole-paper PDFs instead of per-question pages, and it does include solutions.
  • Four question pages are gone for good. Özkan’s three .doc homework files and a handful of her quiz pages were never crawled before the folder was deleted.
  • Recent quizzes rotate. The quizzes page carries the current term only — Fall 2025 as of this build. Earlier terms’ quiz PDFs still sit in the same folder but are no longer linked from anywhere.
  • Checked and empty: the twenty personal folders of every current Mathematics faculty member, the department course index, and a Wayback sweep of the whole fen.bilkent.edu.tr domain. Nothing else public exists for MATH 101.
