Where the search went

Six places, in order, and all of them empty for CS 125.

The department's own course index

w3.cs.bilkent.edu.tr/course-homepages lists every undergraduate CS course with a homepage. It starts at CS 101 and jumps to CS 201. CS 125 is a service course for social-science students and has never been listed there.

Every faculty folder

All twenty-two personal ~user folders on cs.bilkent.edu.tr were fetched and their links searched for the course number. None mentions it. Dayanık, who teaches the department’s other Python service course, has folders for CS 114, CS 115, CS 461 and CS 464 — no CS 125.

Direct path probing

/~user/courses/cs125/ and /~user/teaching/cs125/ were probed across every plausible instructor, plus /~cs125/ as a course-owned folder. All 404.

The Wayback Machine

A CDX sweep of the entire cs.bilkent.edu.tr domain, filtered on every spelling of the course number, returned zero rows. The Internet Archive has never seen a CS 125 file at Bilkent.

Why it is empty

CS 125 is a recent course that has run on Moodle and STARS since it started. Its slides, notebooks and datasets are behind your student login — that is where to get them, on the campus network or the university VPN. Nothing was withheld and nothing was bypassed; the material simply was never posted to the open web.
