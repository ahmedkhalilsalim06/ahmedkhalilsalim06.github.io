ECON 101

Bilkent’s first-year principles course. Five instructors put material online over twenty years. What was searched, and what isn’t here

Search engines index almost none of this material: the files sit loose in personal ~user folders under names like HW4.pdf or mt2.pdf that say nothing about the course, and directory listings are switched off, so nothing can be browsed. What works instead is enumerating the Internet Archive’s index of every file it ever crawled under bilkent.edu.tr — folder by folder, with no keyword filter — and then reading the course pages it returns to find out which course each anonymous filename belonged to. That is how ~cokten/econ101/Midterm1as.doc and ~zaim/week1&2/week1&2.PPT turned up.

ECON 101 — five instructors, one still live

Taner Yiğit’s site survived the department’s move to a new CMS and every file on it was re-tested and still serves today. The folders of Çağla Ökten, Osman Zaim, Selin Sayek Böke and Tarık Kara were deleted when Bilkent retired www.bilkent.edu.tr/~user pages, so everything from them is an archive recovery. Two gaps worth naming: Kara’s three lecture-note PDFs (surplus, theory of the firm, monopoly) are linked from his notes page but were never crawled; and Sayek Böke’s 2003 homework sets 1 and 3–7 and her Fall 2008 sets are in the same position. Both index pages are linked above so you can see what existed. Berument publishes only econometrics, Taşkın only ECON 302 readings, Hasker only ECON 107/203/204/439, and the department’s own site has never hosted anything but discussion papers.

MATH 105 — twelve items, and that is the whole public record

Only Salih Karadağ and Ümit Özger ever posted MATH 105 publicly, and both folders are gone from the server. The course has no public midterm or final — confirmed twice, across the whole fen.bilkent.edu.tr domain, by filename probing as well as keyword search. Everything solved in this subject is MATH 106, which is why it sits in the side panel rather than padding the section out. Gonca Özkan’s Midterm I solutions are linked from her own documents page but were never crawled; Midterms II and III survived.

CS 125 — still genuinely nothing

Rechecked for this update, and the answer did not move. The department course index skips from CS 101 to CS 201, all twenty-two CS faculty folders come back clean, direct path probes return 404, and a full cs.bilkent.edu.tr archive sweep filtered on the course number returns only a professor’s poetry pages and a lab monitoring dashboard. The course has run on Moodle since it started.

Nothing was worked around

Every file here was posted publicly by the instructor. No access control was bypassed; archived copies are snapshots of pages that were openly reachable when crawled. A handful of files that turned up in the sweep were named grade lists and tutorial rosters with student numbers on them — those were found and deliberately left off this page. Current-semester material stays on Moodle and STARS; reach it with your own student login on campus or the university VPN.

Use these the way they were meant

These are study materials. Check each course’s academic-honesty policy before using anything in graded work, and note that the syllabi and grading schemes here belong to old semesters — the Ökten papers are from 2003–06, the Zaim slides from 2001, the Özkan calculus set from 2003.
